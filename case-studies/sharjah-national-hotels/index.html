01 / Project context

The commercial setting.

Five hotel properties with different guest segments and demand patterns, supported by a shared marketing programme over 12 months.

Multi-property marketing coordination, campaign execution and demand generation

02 / The starting point

The business problem.

The group needed efficient acquisition across multiple properties while improving the economics of its booking journey.

03 / Diagnosis

Where the opportunity sat.

A shared group strategy had to preserve property-specific positioning. Campaign structure, landing-page relevance and booking cost needed to be reviewed together.

04 / The thinking

Build around the connection.

Coordinate paid acquisition, property-led creative, conversion journeys and reporting across the portfolio.
