01 / The starting point

The business problem.

Acquisition needed to become more cost-efficient while continuing to generate qualified enquiries.

02 / The thinking

Build around the connection.

Revisit campaign structure and audience targeting, then test creative and use lead-cost, conversion and revenue feedback to guide optimisation.

03 / The work

From strategy to execution.

  1. 01

    Restructured paid-search and paid-social campaigns.

  2. 02

    Refined audiences and tested creative variations.

  3. 03

    Connected acquisition performance with commercial reporting.

  4. 04

    Allocated marketing investment using CPL, conversion and revenue performance.

04 / What the evidence supports

The outcome, in context.

Reduced average CPL from AED 65 to AED 22, reported as a 66% reduction in the CV.

Scaled acquisition to 200–400 qualified leads per month.
