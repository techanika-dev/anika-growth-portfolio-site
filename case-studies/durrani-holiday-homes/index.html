01 / The starting point

The business problem.

The portfolio needed a broader demand mix and stronger coordination between marketing, distribution and commercial decisions.

02 / The thinking

Build around the connection.

Bring customer acquisition, OTA and travel-trade expansion, CRM and performance reporting into an integrated growth programme, working across reservations, revenue, operations and finance.

03 / The work

From strategy to execution.

  1. 01

    Established the marketing function and coordinated cross-functional commercial initiatives.

  2. 02

    Expanded global OTA and demand-channel coverage alongside B2B, wholesale, GDS and travel-trade sources.

  3. 03

    Combined paid acquisition with CRM, direct-booking and remarketing programmes.

  4. 04

    Built AI-enabled automation and commercial reporting workflows to support decisions on marketing investment, pricing and channel mix.

04 / What the evidence supports

The outcome, in context.

Contributed to monthly portfolio revenue growth from AED 504K to AED 1.67M across 60 properties.

Supported occupancy improvement from approximately 45–52% to 89%.

The reported monthly revenue equates to an AED 20M+ annualised run rate; it is not a claim of booked annual revenue.
