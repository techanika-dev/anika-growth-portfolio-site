01 / Project context

The commercial setting.

The Nacravo achievements record describes a six-brand programme across the UAE and Saudi Arabia. The figures below represent that programme, rather than one isolated service campaign.

Growth, paid acquisition and commercial systems within Durrani Group

02 / The starting point

The business problem.

Service demand needed to convert into qualified enquiries and a consistent sales follow-up process across brands, markets and project offers.

03 / Diagnosis

Where the opportunity sat.

Generating more leads would have limited value without clear qualification, consistent landing pages and a shared view of marketing-to-sales progression.

04 / The thinking

Build around the connection.

Combine audience and offer segmentation with dedicated funnels, CRM automation and commercial reporting.

  2. 02

  3. 03

    Developed creative and audience testing across the programme.

  4. 04

    Connected weekly executive dashboards with acquisition and sales-stage reporting.

07 / What the evidence supports

The outcome, in context.

Reported scope: AED 3.9M ad budget, 120+ campaigns, 35+ landing funnels and 20+ CRM automation workflows.

Reported results: 28,000+ qualified leads, 39% lower CPL and 6.4× advertising ROAS.

The record also reports a 46% improvement in lead-to-customer conversion and 58% improvement in conversion rate; these are reported relative improvements.
