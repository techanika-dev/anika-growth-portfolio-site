01 / The starting point

The business problem.

Motion can add personality, but it needs to help visitors understand the work and find the right next step.

02 / The thinking

Build around the connection.

Use a hierarchy-first page structure, restrained transitions and accessible interactions. Treat responsive behaviour and performance as part of the design.

03 / The work

From strategy to execution.

  1. 01

    Create a clear hero, service structure and case-study journey.

  2. 02

    Use progressive reveal, hover feedback and scroll-linked process cues.

  3. 03

    Provide keyboard access and a reduced-motion experience.

  4. 04

    Design distinct desktop, tablet and mobile layouts.

04 / What the evidence supports

A clear design direction.

An interaction direction for future web projects, demonstrated through this portfolio experience.
