Start from the message

  • Choose one audience, one problem, one credible promise and one next action.
  • Use your own approved brand assets and verified evidence.
  • Each brief below can be built in Canva or another design tool; editable Canva files can be connected later.

Creative QA

  • Check mobile text size and placement-safe areas.
  • Match the landing-page promise and CTA.
  • Verify rights for faces, music, logos and testimonials.
  • Keep a record of the hypothesis, audience and success measure.
