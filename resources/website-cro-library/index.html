Website & CRO Optimization Library

A structured review library for service, ecommerce and lead-generation websites.

Practical / working teamsUse with your own business data and approved evidence.

Message & trust

  • State who the page is for, the problem it solves and the next action above the fold.
  • Support claims with relevant evidence and explain what the evidence measures.
  • Answer price, process, suitability and risk questions before asking for a commitment.

Friction review

  • Test the hero, CTA and form on a small phone.
  • Use labelled fields, helpful validation and honest confirmation states.
  • Review heatmaps and session recordings with consent; look for repeated friction rather than isolated behaviour.
  • Check speed, accessibility and conversion events before changing design.

Experiment backlog

  • Write: observation → hypothesis → change → primary metric → guardrail → decision rule.
  • Test service-page intent and enquiry quality, or product information and checkout completion for ecommerce.
  • Do not call a test successful from a small noisy sample or a vanity metric.
