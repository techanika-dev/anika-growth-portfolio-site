Ecommerce / Excel workbook

Complete Ecommerce Business Management Sheet

A formula-driven workbook for revenue, marketing, customer economics and inventory.

Practical / working teamsUse with your own business data and approved evidence.
Download Excel workbook

Workbook coverage

  • Monthly dashboard with revenue, ad spend, ROAS, MER, CAC, AOV, COGS, gross profit and net operating profit.
  • Channel inputs distinguish attributed revenue from total business revenue.
  • Inventory schedule tracks opening stock, receipts, units sold and closing stock.
  • Customer acquisition and repeat-order measures use consistent period inputs.

How to use it

  • Choose one currency and reporting period. Enter amounts excluding sales taxes and net of refunds consistently.
  • Keep attributed channel revenue distinct from total revenue to avoid double counting.
  • Enter new customers and repeat orders from your commerce platform. Review the formula definitions before making decisions.
  • Blank inputs remain blank; no example performance is presented as your results.
