SEO / Checklist

SEO Growth Framework

Connect technical foundations, search intent and organic lead generation.

Practical / working teamsUse with your own business data and approved evidence.

Technical foundation

  • Confirm important pages return useful content, correct status codes and sensible canonical URLs.
  • Review indexability, sitemaps, internal links and mobile usability.
  • Use accurate structured data supported by visible page content.

Search architecture

  • Map services and locations to distinct customer intent.
  • Build topic clusters around useful questions and original expertise.
  • Avoid near-duplicate location pages with no genuine local value.

Commercial measurement

  • Tag enquiry sources and preserve organic landing-page context.
  • Review qualified organic leads and assisted journeys, not ranking changes alone.
  • Prioritise fixes by business relevance, feasibility and evidence.
