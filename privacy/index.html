Privacy information.

Project enquiries

The form asks for contact details and information about your project so Anika can discuss your enquiry. Please avoid including passwords, payment information or sensitive personal data.

How the form works

When direct delivery is configured, your brief is sent to Anika by email through the configured delivery provider. Otherwise, the form prepares a draft for your own email app, with copy and download options. Preparing a draft does not send it.

Use of information

Information you send is used to respond to your enquiry and discuss a potential engagement. Form entries are not saved to this website’s database or browser storage.

Tools and resource libraries

Calculator inputs and imported CSV or workflow files are processed in your browser. They are not uploaded to a server. Favourite resource identifiers are stored on this device in local storage. You can remove favourites in the library or clear this site’s browser data.

Using “Share inputs” includes the selected inputs in a URL that you can choose to share. Anyone with that URL can see those inputs. Creator CSV data is excluded. Avoid including confidential information in shared links.

Booking a call

The calendar loads from Calendly after you choose to open it. Calendly processes booking details, may use cookies and applies its own privacy notice. You can also arrange a call by email.

Site technology

The portfolio itself does not include advertising trackers or marketing cookies. The optional Calendly embed is a third-party service. The hosting and email providers may process technical information necessary to operate their services. External websites, including LinkedIn and portfolio links, have their own privacy practices.

Questions and requests

For a question about information you have shared, including a request to remove it, email anikamehendiratta1@gmail.com.
